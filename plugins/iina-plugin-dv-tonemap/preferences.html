Why do Dolby Vision Profile 5 videos look purple or green?
Profile 5 uses the proprietary IPTPQc2 color space with SMPTE ST 2084 (PQ) transfer curves instead of standard YCbCr. Players without an active DOVI reshaper misinterpret IPT as YCbCr, causing intense cyan/magenta distortion. This plugin hooks into the rendering pipeline and applies a calibrated hardware matrix to accurately map IPTPQc2 to Rec.709 SDR.
